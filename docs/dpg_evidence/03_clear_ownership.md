## 3.1 What the DPG Standard requires

Ownership of everything the project produces (source code, content and data) must be clearly defined and documented. The usual evidence is a copyright or trademark statement, or another public record that names the owner, together with a public URL where a reviewer can verify it.

## 3.3 What is owned

- **Source code:** the Flask website and API (qumanity-web) and the React Native / Expo phone app (qumanity-app), both licensed to the public under AGPL-3.0 (see Document 02).
- **Documentation:** the design and build notes in each repository's `docs/` folder, the White Paper v4.0, the Technical Architecture v4.0 and this evidence pack (`docs/dpg_evidence/` in qumanity-web).
- **Brand:** the name "Qumanity" and the logo files in `static/images/` (`logo-qumanity*.svg`) and `static/images/_archive/` (`logo-qumanity.png`, an earlier raster version kept for reference, superseded by the SVG set).
- **Content and compiled data:** interface translations in 12 languages (`static/locales/`), lunar month names (`translations/lunar_months.json`) and the location hierarchy compiled for the Zone-to-Village structure.

Third-party components keep their own licences and owners (Document 02, section 2.6). The marquee poem translations are not owned by Qumanity (section 3.4).

## 3.4 Marquee poems provenance

The marquee verses come from the Vinay Patrika of Goswami Tulsidas (d. 1623), which is in the public domain. The Hindi translations previously used were prepared by Shri Hanuman Prasad Poddar, who died in 1971. Under section 22 of the Copyright Act, 1957, copyright lasts for the author's lifetime plus 60 years, counted from the year after death, so these translations remain in copyright in India until the end of 2031 and expire on 1 January 2032. No cleanly-licensed replacement is currently available.

The marquee has therefore been disabled on both the website and the phone app as of 7 October 2026, behind the feature flags `QM_MARQUEE_ENABLED` (web) and `EXPO_PUBLIC_QM_MARQUEE_ENABLED` (app), both off by default. The poem files have been moved out of the public repository and are preserved on the Founder's local machine. The rendering code stays in the repository. The marquee will be re-enabled only with a cleanly-licensed or work-for-hire translation, or with the Poddar text after 1 January 2032. Re-enablement steps are in `docs/marquee_poems_archive/README.md`.

## 3.5 Earth imagery provenance

The 3D globe on the website uses `static/images/earth_texture.png`, generated by `scripts/build_world_texture.py` from Natural Earth data, which is in the public domain. Two older images, `earth3.jpeg` and `earth13.png`, are still tracked in the repository but are not referenced by any page. Their source is not documented, so they are scheduled for removal (section 3.6) rather than claimed as owned.
