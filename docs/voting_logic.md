# Voting

## Posts
- Vote **+1, 0 or −1**, **once per post** until the voting window ends (changing your vote is allowed until then).
- Only people **presently living** in a group's location can vote there, and only on **that** group's pages. Birth-village members read only. Nobody votes on other pages, but everybody can read statistics of any place ([sub_groups.md](sub_groups.md)).
- Windows and lift scores:

| Level | Days | Elevate at score ≥ |
|---|---|---|
| Village | 7 | 10 |
| Tehsil | 8 | 100 |
| District | 9 | 1,000 |
| State | 10 | 10,000 |
| Zone | 11 | 100,000 |
| Country | 12 | 1,000,000 |
| Continent | 13 | 10,000,000 |
| Earth | 14 | (100,000,000 = Earth honour) freezes |

At the end: **elevate** (score ≥ lift) · **freeze** (positive but below) · **remove** (zero or negative → Private timeline). Comments open only after voting ends. Code: `board_core.py` (website), same rules in `Qumanity_App/docs/DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md` §8.
- Freezing pays the author ([post_rewards.md](post_rewards.md)).

## Council elections (separate from post voting)
- **Who may vote:** everyone living in the place aged **5 or over** — Balak (from 5), Yuvak, Vridh; male, female and third-gender members.
- **Who may stand:** Yuvak or Vridh, **up to age 60**, for every level Village → Earth. Balak cannot stand.
- **Third-gender members** (born one gender, presently living as the other): they vote using their **present** gender — a present-male votes in the male list, a present-female in the female list — matched to the **sign/element they were born under** (unchanged; a zodiac sign always comes from date of birth). They may vote from age 5 up, the same as everyone else, but **cannot stand as a candidate** — candidates are Yuvak/Vridh who are pure male or pure female. *(Decided by the founder, 2026-09-27.)*
- **Matching:** a voter votes only for candidates of their **own sign/element** (Fire/Aries voters → Fire/Aries candidates) and of their **own gender** (men → male, women → female candidates). Same for all 12 signs and both genders.
- **Village:** one vote per person per **year**, on their **birthday's sun-sign** cycle; men vote for male candidates, women for female candidates of their sign. Each year the village elects **24** leaders (12 signs × male/female). Only people living there vote.
- **Tehsil → Earth:** the level below elects **8** (Fire, Earth, Air, Water × male/female). Electors are the council members of the child groups.
- **Minimum residents before elections start:** Village 100 · Tehsil 1,000 · District 10,000 · State 100,000 · Zone 100,000 · Country 1,000,000 · Continent 1,000,000 · Earth 10,000,000.
- A leader serves **only their sign's month**, then is released and may stand again or stand for the next council up: [qpunch_council.md](qpunch_council.md).
- A new user's first council vote **unlocks** their registration Qoins ([qoins_earn_reference.md](qoins_earn_reference.md)).
