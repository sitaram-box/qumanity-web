Forgot your Private ID?

Three ways to recover it, depending on what you still remember.

For when you've forgotten both your Private ID and your PIN. We'll send a one-time code to a parent, child, sibling, or spouse's phone who is already a verified family member on your Family Tree -- not to you.

⚠️ This is a manual process reviewed by our team — it is not instant, and your Private ID is only ever sent by physical post, never electronically.
