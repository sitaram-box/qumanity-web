Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

Qumanity is stewarded by its founder, Rohit Mudgal. Questions about your data can be sent any time to founder@qumanity.in.

What we collect

  • Your name, gender, date/time/place of birth, and your current location — used to create your account and calculate your personal kundali (Sun sign, Moon sign, element).
  • A PIN, which we never store as typed — it is run through a one-way scrambling process (a "hash") before it ever touches our database, so even we can't see your actual PIN.
  • Anything you choose to post, message, or upload — board posts, Qmail messages, family tree entries, profile pictures.
  • Optionally, an email address or phone number, if you choose to give one.

Why we collect it

  • To verify who you are and let you sign in.
  • To work out which village, tehsil, district, or country your account belongs to, so your civic features (voting, your village's board) go to the right place.
  • To calculate your kundali and elemental details, which some of Qumanity's features use.
  • To let you message, post, and connect with other members.

Who can see it

  • Your private information (kundali, personal notes, your birth details) is visible only to you.
  • What you post to your village's board is visible to other members of that location, as a matter of public record — that's the point of a public village board.
  • A public member directory shows only your name, gender, and an age band — never your exact birthdate, your address, or your contact details.
  • Qumanity's own administrator can see full account records when needed to help you, investigate abuse, or respond to a legal request. Every time that happens, it is logged.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads, and we don't track you across other websites.
  • We don't sell your data to anyone, ever.
  • We don't process real money. "Qoins" are an internal record of contribution, not currency — there is no bank account or payment processor behind them.

Your choices

You can ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by writing to founder@qumanity.in.

If you'd like your data deleted, write to founder@qumanity.in and the founder will handle your request by hand. There is no automated "delete my account" button today — closing an account otherwise marks it inactive rather than erasing its history, to keep the public record honest for everyone else. A deletion request is handled as its own, individually reviewed case.

Children

Qumanity does not currently check anyone's age at registration. There is no minimum age enforced today.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so clearly on this page, with the date above updated.
