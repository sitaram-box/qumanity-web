        How it works

        Three ledgers. One living system.

        Reality is spatially nested, temporally cyclical and consciously intentional. Qumanity models all three — and binds them together.

        Nested containers, from Earth to you

        Every action happens inside a defined container — and each belongs to a larger one. A village’s entry automatically belongs to every container above it, so nothing hides in the gaps.

        • Location is the primary index — codes embed their parents
        • Authority flows up, accountability flows down
        • Each container controls the records made in its jurisdiction

        Time that follows the cosmos, not the campaign

        Actions are stamped inside cycles — elements, zodiac months, lunar fortnights, days — so the system recognises patterns and aligns governance with natural rhythm.

        The Qoin economy

        Goodness, made measurable.

        Qoins are ledger-tagged units of verified contribution — not a speculative coin. They can’t be traded on open markets or lent at interest; they are earned, received and spent within Qumanity.

        1. 1

          Action

          Teach, plant, care, restore, mediate, create.

        2. 2

        Built on trust

        Safeguards are the architecture, not an afterthought.

        AI is advisory only

        S(AI) reads the ledgers and advises. Binding decisions stay with human councils, on open, auditable algorithms.

        Privacy by design

        You own your data. Private timelines are encrypted; sharing is by revocable consent. Public posts show only village-level location.

        Voluntary & incremental

        Adoption is opt-in, with a long coexistence period alongside existing systems. Trust is earned, never decreed.

        Culturally configurable

        A coordination protocol, not a cultural template. Communities define what counts as value and keep their customs.

        Built for the last village

        Solar-powered, offline-capable kiosks — no smartphone, constant internet or literacy required.

        Open & non-proprietary

        Stewarded by a non-profit foundation. No shareholders, no data monetisation, no owner.

        Roadmap

        India-first. Sub-continent now. Planetary next.

        With more than 620,000 villages and existing digital public infrastructure, India is the ultimate stress-test. If it works there, it can work anywhere.

        1. You are herePhase 1

          Foundation

          Architecture, interdisciplinary team, partnerships — and a working prototype of identity, timelines, wallets and pilot elections.

        2. Phase 2

          Prototype district

          A full-stack deployment across about 1,000 villages: kiosks, onboarding, the ledgers, Qoins and councils.

        3. Phase 3

          State scale

          50–100 million people: scaling laws, interoperability and cross-jurisdiction flows.

        4. Phase 4

          Planetary framework

          Modular adoption for other nations — voluntary, sovereign, culturally adapted.
